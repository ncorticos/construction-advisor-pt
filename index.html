Construction Advisor PTSCE Envelope + Budget

Where are you building?

Portuguese thermal regulation classifies a site from two things only: the NUTS III region it falls in, and its altitude. Every reference parameter is tabulated for a reference altitude and corrected linearly from there. Name the municipality and the height above sea level and this application resolves the winter and summer zones, then opens a mosaic of materials — natural and industrialized, relevant to the area — before working through the envelope element by element against the limits actually in force.

Grounded in the regulation, not in a rule of thumb

Reference climate by NUTS III and its altitude slopes, maximum thermal transmittance by zone and region, admissible solar factor by inertia, ground-floor coefficients by characteristic dimension — read from the Manual SCE and the Portaria, and cited where used.

Assemble the element, read the number

Build a roof, a floor, a wall layer by layer from a materials library and watch the thermal transmittance resolve against the limit for your zone. The insulation thickness that closes the gap is reported directly.

Mainland, Madeira and the Azores

All thirty NUTS III regions are carried, with the separate limits the Autonomous Regions are given. Nothing outside the region you name is shown, so what you read is the answer for your site and not an average of the country.

From the U-value to the budget

Every envelope solution carries quantities and a cost: enter the area and the unit price read from the Gerador de Preços and the application totals each element and the overall budget, so the thermal choice is always read together with what it costs.
